Join Us

We are always looking for motivated researchers to join us at Beihang University. Our work spans robotics, medical devices, and embodied AI. If you are passionate about making robots that help people, we would love to hear from you.

Undergraduate Research Opportunities

We welcome motivated undergraduates to join our lab for research experience. What we offer:

  • Opportunity to publish in high-impact journals as a co-author
  • Support and encouragement to participate in academic competitions
  • Strong recommendation letters that provide a competitive advantage for graduate school admission

To apply, please send an email to yanggangfeng@buaa.edu.cn with a brief introduction of yourself and your research interests.
